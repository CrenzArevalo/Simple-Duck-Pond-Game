❓ How to play

🕹️ Catching ducks

Move your net with the mouse (or your finger). Click / tap the water and the net will scoop. Any duck inside the net when it dips gets caught.

Ducks are shy — they'll paddle away when the net gets close. Herd them toward the shore to corner them!

Every duck you catch tumbles straight into your 🪣 bucket in the corner of the pond.

🧺 Visitors & coins

Catching alone doesn't pay — you earn coins by selling ducks. Visitors wander up to the pond every so often; when one arrives, a Sell button appears above them. Selling a duck earns you 6 coins. You can also sell the duck you have selected in 🐣 My Ducks.

Some ducks are worth more: rare ducks (like the Lagoon) and the shimmering golden duck sell for double — 12 coins. 💎

🥚 The nest

A nest of duck eggs sits by the pond. The eggs wobble, crack open, and the ducklings waddle straight into the water — then you can catch them like any other duck.

💎 Rare ducks

Most ducks are worth 6 coins, but rare ducks (and the dazzling golden duck) pay double — 12 coins. You'll spot a golden duck easily: it shines brightly, glowing with a warm halo and twinkling sparks. Only one golden duck appears per game, so catch it if you can!

🛍️ The Store

Open the store with the Store button (or press B) to buy cute hats, glasses, scarves and toys. Items you buy go into your wardrobe and can be worn by any duck.

🎨 Duck patterns

Every duck wears its own coat — polka dots, stripes, waves, checker, hearts, stars, speckles or a glowing radiant gradient — in all sorts of colours.

🐣 Dressing up

Open My Ducks (or press D). Pick a duck, drag the preview to spin it, and click a wardrobe item to put it on. You can release a dressed duck back into the pond so it swims around in style.

🔊 Sound

A gentle piano soundtrack plays while you paddle. The ducks themselves stay quiet as they swim, and every duck you scoop into the bucket gives a little quack over the music.
